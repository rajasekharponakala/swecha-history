# Swecha Historical Timeline

A chronological, source-backed timeline of the Swecha organisation and its projects, 2002–2026.

> Dates are recorded only where a public source states them. Recurring programmes are represented by documented editions rather than an invented complete year-by-year list.

## 2002 — Pre-history

- **Book release / Free Software global standards milestone.** A Swecha-history document surfaces a 2002 milestone described as "Book Release (2002) — Free Software Global Standards — Fonts & Translations — Custom build OS". The available public extract does not identify the book or establish that Swecha already existed as an organisation, so this is best treated as pre-history rather than the founding date.
  - Source: [Scribd — "Swecha OS: A Telugu Free Software Journey"](https://www.scribd.com/document/816774415/swecha-stroy)

## 2003 — Fonts and Unicode Telugu development

- **Fonts & Unicode Telugu development.** Need for an alternative to proprietary (ANU) fonts; no Telugu fonts available at the time. Work with George Williams on Telugu support in pfaedit (now FontForge).
  - Source: [Scribd — Swecha story](https://www.scribd.com/document/816774415/swecha-stroy)

## 2004 — Telugu in the digital world

- **First Telugu support for the Pango rendering engine**, led by the Free Software team.
- **GNOME with Telugu — birth of Swecha OS** as a Free Software OS alternative.
  - Source: [Scribd — Swecha story](https://www.scribd.com/document/816774415/swecha-stroy)

## 2005 — Founding and first release

- **Swecha founded (2005).** Founded as an organisation to support the Swecha project. Objective: a complete computing solution for Telugu-speaking users, bridging the English-language digital divide through Free Software localisation.
  - Sources: [swecha.org/about](https://swecha.org/about), [swecha.org/content/swecha-project](https://swecha.org/content/swecha-project), [Wikipedia — Swecha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swecha)
- **First Swecha test release — 8 February 2005.** Released by Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister Y. S. Rajasekhara Reddy in the presence of Richard M. Stallman (FSF). Followed work on a GPL Telugu font, a shared glossary and translation of core applications/UI.
  - Source: [swecha.org/about](https://swecha.org/about)
- **Swecha is a member of the Free Software Movement of India (FSMI).** Formerly called Free Software Foundation, Andhra Pradesh (FSF-AP).
  - Source: [Wikipedia — Swecha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swecha)

## 2005–2016 — Swecha OS / Telugu localisation as a Live distribution

- Swecha OS provided a GNOME desktop localised into Telugu, Telugu text-to-speech integrated with the desktop, and a LiveCD. Independent archive records Swecha as Ubuntu-based, x86/x86_64, Unity desktop, Live DVD; last listed version released 2016.
  - Sources: [swecha.org/content/swecha-project](https://swecha.org/content/swecha-project), [archiveos.org/swecha](https://archiveos.org/swecha/), [DistroWatch — Swecha](https://distrowatch.com/swecha)

## Undated (documented in public history) — BalaSwecha

- **BalaSwecha** conceived as a school-focused Free Software project for elementary through tenth-standard learners, with interactive applications for maths, physics, geography and chemistry, plus teacher/computer-instructor training. Live site reports 150+ applications/simulations, 100+ syllabus-mapped videos and the Pencilbox chapter/quiz system; deployments across Telugu-speaking districts; 10.04 and 14.04 downloads.
  - Sources: [Wikipedia — Swecha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swecha), [balaswecha.in](https://balaswecha.in/), [swechaap.org/initiatives/balaswecha](https://www.swechaap.org/initiatives/balaswecha/)

## 2024 — AI Yatra and Summer of AI

- **Summer of AI / AI Creators Program — 12 August 2024 (announced).** Two-week online programme (daily 18:00–20:00) to build Telugu LLM capability, TTS/voice avatars and a Telugu corpus (folk tales, traditional skills); top performers could receive Swecha fellowships. Partners: Swecha, IIIT Hyderabad, Ozonetel, TASK.
  - Source: [swecha.org/summer-of-ai](https://swecha.org/summer-of-ai)
- **Telugu AI Yatra / Telangana Vaibhavam AI Program — October–November 2024.** Swecha with Government of Telangana, TASK, IIIT Hyderabad, Ozonetel and Tech Vedika ran AI Yatra across Telangana colleges. Two-hour sessions to demystify AI and collect Telugu corpus material; schedule names 33 districts and dated stops: Mahabubnagar (21 Oct), Jadcharla (22 Oct), Gadwal (23 Oct), Kalwakurthy/Suryapet (24 Oct), Karimnagar/Narayanpet/Sangareddy/Siddipet (25–26 Oct), Warangal (5 Nov), Nizamabad (6 Nov).
  - Source: [swecha.org/ai-yatra](https://swecha.org/ai-yatra)
- **WomenWhoHack — November 2024 (23 Nov).** Hyderabad session on data engineering (raw data to insights), networking and live demo. Initiative aims to increase women's participation in technology, build solidarity/upskilling/mentoring network, develop women tech speakers/leaders contributing to FOSS.
  - Source: [swecha.org/WomenWhoHack](https://swecha.org/WomenWhoHack)
- **Emerging Tech Focus** period (Swecha public history milestone).

## 2025

- **DevDays Hyderabad AI/ML — 5 April 2025.** In-person session at Micron Technology Aquila on Model Context Protocol and agentic AI in decision automation, with talks/live demos.
  - Source: [events.swecha.org/DevDays/2025-devdayshyd-ai-5](https://events.swecha.org/DevDays/2025-devdayshyd-ai-5/)
- **VISWAM.ai and community corpus/data infrastructure (2025–2026).** VISWAM.ai presented as a joint Swecha–IIIT Hyderabad Centre of Excellence on AI for the Global South. Initiatives: Gonthuka Telugu ASR, AI Chandamama Kathalu, Swecha AI OS. Datasets: grassroots cultural data, Telugu documents (estimated ~50 lakh pages), Gonthuka dataset with 1,200+ hours and 20,000+ volunteers; categories include folk tales/songs, local history, food/agriculture, education and medical-camp data. DevSprint materials link the Indic Corpus application and API as part of Mission Indian LLMs.
  - Sources: [viswam.ai](https://viswam.ai/), [viswam.ai/datasets](https://viswam.ai/datasets), [swecha.org/devsprints](https://swecha.org/devsprints)

## 2026

- **DevSprints — health-tech and Indic Corpus hackathon — 5 July 2026.** In-person DevSprint at Jiva Infotech focused on upstream contributions. Projects: Medical Camp system (digitising a service reported at 22,000+ consultations) and Indic Corpus application for Mission Indian LLMs (React/FastAPI), with source repositories and corpus API documentation.
  - Source: [swecha.org/devsprints](https://swecha.org/devsprints)
- **BuildAI4India Free DevDays Workshop — 15 August 2026.** Full-day Free AI Workbench workshop at Swecha's Gachibowli office. Topics: local containerised AI environment, Swecha CLI, open-source models, AI applications, Indian-language AI, freedom to run/understand/modify the stack without vendor lock-in.
  - Source: [events.swecha.org/DevDays/2026-devdays-3](https://events.swecha.org/DevDays/2026-devdays-3/)
- **VISWAM.ai Summer of AI (2026).** Open to undergraduate engineering students, primarily remote, four weeks/cohorts. Combines AI/ML and Telugu ASR training with corpus collection, regional interviews about history/customs/folk culture, validation, mini-projects and a final presentation. Certificates issued by Swecha with IIIT Hyderabad and Meta.
  - Sources: [viswam.ai/summer-of-ai](https://viswam.ai/summer-of-ai), [viswam.ai](https://viswam.ai/)
- **VISWAM.ai roadmap — 2026 'Grow' phase** for talent, products, startups, research institutes and AI services, following a 2024 establishment phase, preceding 2028 consolidation and 2030 Global South AI hub goals.
  - Source: [viswam.ai](https://viswam.ai/)
- **Swecha AI OS** direction: builds on Swecha OS, BalaSwecha and E-Swecha to deliver a next-generation open-source OS with AI capabilities at its core (Debian-based live-build). Repository created 31 July 2020.
  - Source: [code.swecha.org/debian/swecha-os](https://code.swecha.org/debian/swecha-os)

## FSMI and public policy milestones

- **2010-03-21 — FSMI formed.** Free Software Movement of India announced at the valedictory of the National Free Software Conference 2010, Bangalore. Swecha is a member organisation.
- **2010 — NCAR 2010** hosted by FSMI; A. P. J. Abdul Kalam inaugurated.
- **2011-12 — NCAR 2011** hosted by FSMI; Kalam urged extending the free-software community model to research.
- **2012-06-09 — Internet Freedom protest** called by FSMI; police scuttled Hyderabad protest; activists including Y. Kiran Chandra taken into custody.
- **2013 — AICTE rescinds Microsoft Office 365 mandate** after intervention by groups led by FSMI.
- **2014-01 — Swecha policy demands:** hardware manufacturing, data centres, software design centres; critique of IT Act Section 66A protests.
- **2015-06 — Airtel 3G script injection** criticised by FSMI.
- **2015-09 — Software patents / Computer Related Inventions** guidelines opposed by FSMI.
- **2014-01 — Surveillance seminars** (Richard Stallman, Neville Roy Singham) organised by Swecha.

See [`fsmi-and-public-policy.md`](fsmi-and-public-policy.md) for full details.
