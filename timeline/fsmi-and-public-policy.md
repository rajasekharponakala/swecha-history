# Free Software Movement of India (FSMI) and Public Policy

This document extends the Swecha history with the **larger Free Software Movement of India (FSMI)**, of which Swecha (Telangana & Andhra Pradesh) is a member organisation, and with the **public-policy advocacy** carried out by Swecha/FSMI.

## FSMI — overview

- **Free Software Movement of India (FSMI)** is a national coalition of regional and sectoral free-software movements operating across India.
- **Formation:** announced at the valedictory function of the National Free Software Conference 2010, held in Bangalore on 20–21 March 2010.
- **Type:** coalition of organisations; grass-roots, mass-movement style.
- **Region served:** Indian Union.
- **President:** Prabir Purkayastha. **General Secretary:** Kiran Chandra Yarlagadda.
- **Main organ:** General Council.
- **Website:** https://www.fsmi.in/
- **Swecha's place:** listed as a regional movement — "Swecha, Telangana & Andhra Pradesh".

Source: [Wikipedia — Free Software Movement of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Software_Movement_of_India)

## Governance

- FSMI elects the General Council, Executive Committee and office bearers (President, General Secretary) at national conferences.
- Founding conference elected a General Council of 69 members and an Executive Committee of 28 members; Joseph Thomas was founding President and Kiran Chandra Yarlagadda founding General Secretary.
- Second national conference (4Ccon) held at B.S. Abdur Rahman University, Vandalur, Chennai; Prabir Purkayastha elected President, Kiran Chandra Yarlagadda re-elected General Secretary.

## Objectives

- Propagate the ideology of free software and popularise its use.
- Take free software and its ideological implications to computer users "across the digital divide", to under-privileged sections of society.

## Public policy advocacy (FSMI + Swecha)

### AICTE / Microsoft Office 365 mandate (2013)

- AICTE had made proprietary Office 365 mandatory in all engineering colleges. After intervention by groups led by FSMI, the mandate was rescinded.
- Sources: The Hindu (9 May 2013), Wikipedia — FSMI.

### Software patents / Computer Related Inventions (2015)

- FSMI alleged that new Guidelines for Examination of Computer Related Inventions were illogical and violated the spirit and law of the amended Patents Act, 1970; permitting software patents through the backdoor would restrict innovation and impact youth creativity/entrepreneurship.
- Source: The Hindu Business Line (7 Sep 2015).

### Airtel 3G script injection (2015)

- FSMI criticised Airtel for tampering with users' online communications on its 3G network and injecting advertisements into user data, violating privacy rights; appealed to net-neutrality groups to oppose Airtel and Flash Networks.
- Source: FSMI press release (June 2015, archived).

### Internet freedom protest (9 June 2012)

- FSMI gave a national call to protest for Internet Freedom; in Hyderabad police scuttled a planned protest; activists including Y. Kiran Chandra (General Secretary, FSMI) were taken into custody, then shifted to Indira Park.
- Source: The Hindu Business Line (June 2012).

### NCAR 2010 / 2011 — National Convention for Academics and Research

- Organised by FSMI; inaugurated by former President Dr A. P. J. Abdul Kalam, who urged extending the free-software community model to research in health care, agriculture, energy and safe drinking water.
- Source: The Hindu (Dec 2010), abdulkalam.com.

### Swecha-specific policy demands

- Swecha demanded that Central and State governments bring policy changes in information technology to give fillip to hardware manufacturing, setting up of data centres and software design centres; cited India's lack of mobile manufacturing units despite a ~Rs. 16,000 crore annual mobile market.
- Source: The Hindu (Jan 2014).

### IT Act Section 66A protests (2012)

- Swecha was in the forefront of protests in Hyderabad against Section 66(A) of the Information Technology Act following arrests over a Facebook comment.
- Source: The Hindu (Dec 2012).

### AICTE / Microsoft deal representation

- Swecha was in the forefront of protests against inclusion of proprietary software; FSMI made representation to AICTE against the deal with Microsoft.
- Source: fsmi.in (archived), The Hindu Business Line (Apr 2014).

### Digital snooping / surveillance

- Swecha organised seminars on free software and internet freedom where Richard Stallman, Neville Roy Singham and others warned about surveillance; Swecha framed internet surveillance as a threat to democracy.
- Source: The Hindu (Jan 2014).

## Controversy

- A Mathrubhumi report alleged FSMI was under the tutelage of certain political parties and had split the free-software movement by sidetracking FSFI; FSMI published a rejoinder criticising the report for not contacting FSMI leadership. The report also noted Kiran Chandra's association with Novell/FSFI board.
- Sources: Mathrubhumi (Mar 2010, archived), Deshabhimani, FSMI rejoinder.
