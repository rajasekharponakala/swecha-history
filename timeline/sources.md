## Notes on dating

- Dates are recorded only where a public source states them.
- The 2002 entry is treated as pre-history; the public extract does not establish Swecha as an organisation at that time.
- Recurring programmes (summer camps, DevDays) are represented by documented editions rather than a complete year-by-year list.
- Some beta.swecha.org pages were unavailable during collection; their content was cross-checked via search snippets.
